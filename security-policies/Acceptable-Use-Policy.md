## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) establishes the rules and guidelines for the appropriate use of [Organization Name]'s information systems, networks, and data assets. This policy aims to protect the organization, its employees, and its partners from unauthorized or harmful actions.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and other personnel who access the organization's information systems and resources, including but not limited to:

- Desktop and laptop computers
- Mobile devices (phones, tablets)
- Network infrastructure
- Email and messaging systems
- Cloud-based applications and services
- Remote access connections

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 General Use

- Information systems are provided primarily for business purposes.
- Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job duties or violate any policies.
- Users are responsible for the security of their credentials and must not share passwords.

### 3.2 Acceptable Use

Users MAY:
- Access systems and data necessary for their job functions
- Use email for business communications
- Access the internet for work-related research
- Store work files on approved cloud storage platforms

### 3.3 Prohibited Activities

Users MUST NOT:
- Access, download, or distribute unauthorized or illegal content
- Install unauthorized software or hardware on organization systems
- Share login credentials with any other person
- Use organization systems to conduct personal business ventures
- Attempt to bypass security controls or access restricted systems
- Connect unauthorized devices to the organization's network
- Send unsolicited bulk emails or spam
- Store sensitive data on unapproved personal devices or cloud services

### 3.4 Email and Communication

- Email should be used responsibly and professionally.
- Users must not open suspicious attachments or click unknown links.
- Confidential information must be encrypted when transmitted externally.
- Auto-forwarding of organization email to external accounts is prohibited.

### 3.5 Remote Access

- Remote access must use organization-approved VPN connections.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access.
- Users must ensure their remote workspace prevents unauthorized viewing of sensitive information.

### 3.6 Data Protection

- Users must classify and handle data according to the organization's data classification policy.
- Sensitive data must not be stored on removable media without encryption.
- Data must be backed up according to established backup procedures.

## 4. Enforcement

| Violation Level | Example | Potential Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Excessive personal internet use | Verbal warning |
| Moderate | Installing unauthorized software | Written warning |
| Serious | Sharing credentials with others | Suspension of access |
| Critical | Intentional data breach or theft | Termination and legal action |

## 5. Compliance Monitoring

The organization reserves the right to monitor the use of its information systems to ensure compliance with this policy. Monitoring may include:

- Network traffic analysis
- Email content scanning
- System access log reviews
- Periodic audits of user activity

## 6. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in the organization's IT environment or regulatory requirements.
